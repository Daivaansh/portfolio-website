---About me---

Hello, I’m Daivaansh.

I am a Computer Science and Engineering student at Sir M. Visvesvaraya Institute of Technology, and I am also pursuing a BBA in Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at IIM Bangalore.

I work at the intersection of technology, business and finance, with interests spanning entrepreneurship, business development, strategic partnerships, fintech, investment banking and quantitative finance.

I’m fascinated by the mechanics behind businesses and financial markets — how capital moves, how technology creates opportunities, how data drives decisions and how strategy translates into competitive advantage.

I approach problems with an engineer’s analytical mindset and a business-oriented perspective. I value ideas, but I’m more interested in their execution, scalability and ability to create measurable value.

Beyond academics: student leadership, executive support, relationship-building and strategic initiatives.

I don’t just look at how things work. I look at what makes them work better, scale faster and create value.
